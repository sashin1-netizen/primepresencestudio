## Council conclusion

Strongest sections: hero, strategic framing, Services, Process, final CTA/contact. Weakest commercial surfaces: empty Work, pending Insights, absent founder/proof. No cinematic section is decorative enough to remove; each supports positioning, service understanding or conversion. The site is suitable for controlled private prospect review, not broad launch or paid traffic.
