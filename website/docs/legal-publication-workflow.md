# Legal publication workflow

For Privacy, Terms, cookie determination, analytics determination, portfolio wording, media wording and contact-data handling: retain owner-supplied draft text, obtain professional review where appropriate, record reviewer, version, approval date and outstanding issues, then set approval. Approval alone does not publish: each page remains 404 and absent from navigation/sitemap until its publication status is `published` and its version/date are recorded.

Revisions reset the affected status to `draft` or `review`. Never infer legal entity, jurisdiction, retention, lawful basis, rights or compliance. Run `npm run owner:validate` and route checks after every status change.
