# Business details form

Purpose: confirm the single source used by contact, footer, legal, metadata and structured data. Public launch is blocked until the owner and legal reviewer approve applicable fields.

## Required owner response

- Public business name and exact legal contracting name
- Public email, telephone and WhatsApp in international format
- Public location wording, service area and operating hours
- Approved HTTPS social links with labels
- Copyright holder; legal, privacy and terms contacts
- Production domain, canonical HTTPS URL and www/non-www preference
- Owner name, approval date and written approval reference

Recommended lengths: location/service-area 2–12 words; public description 25–45 words. Example structure: `[public name] / [legal entity] / [contact] / [approved URL]`; bracketed text is instructional, not real data. No address or registration detail should be supplied unless accurate and appropriate for publication.

Formats: UTF-8 text, HTTPS URLs, international telephone numbers. Rights: owner must have authority to publish the contact and social data. Set content to `review`, then `approved`, and only mark supporting public records `published` after final approval.
