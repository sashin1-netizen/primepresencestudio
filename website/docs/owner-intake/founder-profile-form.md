# Founder profile form

Purpose: supply human credibility for About. The section remains hidden until publication and portrait-rights gates pass.

- Name, role and location wording
- Short biography: 80–120 words; long biography: 180–300 words
- Experience summary: 3–5 evidence-backed points
- Approach: 40–70 words; values: 3–5 concise items
- Optional approved HTTPS social links
- Portrait: 1200×1500 WebP/AVIF/JPEG, preferably ≤250 KB, meaningful alt text
- Portrait creator/source, copyright holder, licence/assignment and written permission
- Approval date and status: `draft`, `review`, `approved`, `published` or `rejected`

Example structure: `[Name] is [role]. [Relevant experience]. [Approach and client value].` Do not add awards, years, clients or credentials without evidence. Founder approval and image-rights confirmation are both required before `published`.
