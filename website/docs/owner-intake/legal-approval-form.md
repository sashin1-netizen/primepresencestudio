# Legal approval form

Purpose: record professional review; this form is not legal advice.

- Privacy policy approved: yes/no; reviewer and date
- Terms approved: yes/no; reviewer and date
- Cookie-policy determination and reasoning
- Analytics/consent determination
- Approved media-use and client-project publication wording
- Responsible legal, privacy and terms contacts
- Outstanding issues and written approval reference

Example structure: `[document/version] reviewed by [qualified reviewer] on [date]; [approved/changes required]; [open issues]`. Public launch remains blocked while an issue is open or approval/reviewer/date is missing.
