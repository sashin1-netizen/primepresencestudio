# Team profile form

Purpose: identify genuine delivery participants on About without implying employment or credentials incorrectly.

- Name, accurate role/relationship and 50–90 word biography
- Optional location, experience points and HTTPS social links
- Portrait: 1200×1500 WebP/AVIF/JPEG, preferably ≤250 KB, descriptive alt text
- Individual publication consent plus creator/source, copyright and image-rights evidence
- Approval date and publication status

Example: `[Name] supports [accurate discipline/relationship]. [Relevant, evidenced contribution].` A missing team is acceptable; the section stays hidden. `published`, an approval date and confirmed image rights are mandatory to render.
