# Testimonial approval form

Purpose: collect truthful social proof with traceable consent.

- Exact client name, optional role and business name
- Exact quote, recommended 25–70 words
- Related project slug or documented reason the reference is withheld
- Written approval reference, consent date and approver authority
- Publication status

Example structure: `“[Exact client-supplied words].” — [Name], [role], [business]`. Do not rewrite meaning or invent attribution. Empty quotes, missing consent, missing approval dates and non-published statuses never render. Project-less testimonials require an explicit editorial/legal decision in the approval record and never generate unsupported structured-review data.
