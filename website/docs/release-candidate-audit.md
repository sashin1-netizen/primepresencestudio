# Release-candidate product audit

Date: 4 August 2026. Evidence: fresh production-build screenshots in `docs/artifacts/release-audit/` at 390×844 and 1440×900. All audited pages had one H1, completed loading, retained the dark visual system and showed no horizontal overflow or application error fallback.

## Flow

1. **Home — healthy visual entry, commercially incomplete.** The hero communicates category, tone and two clear next actions without crowding mobile. The primary weakness is downstream: “View Our Work” leads to an honest empty state rather than proof.
2. **Services — healthy information architecture.** The mobile hierarchy is clear and outputs make each service tangible. Improvement: add owner-approved fit, typical range and engagement boundaries once confirmed; these will reduce unsuitable enquiries.
3. **Contact — visually healthy, operationally blocked.** The form is legible and responsive, but the configured destination email is invalid, business details are unconfirmed and the privacy policy is unapproved. Submission is correctly disabled. The visible WhatsApp number also requires owner confirmation before public promotion.
4. **Work — honest but the largest conversion gap.** The empty state avoids fabricated proof and provides two useful paths. A single strong, approved case study will have more impact than additional animation or decorative sections.

## Prioritized improvements

1. **P0 — restore a trustworthy conversion path:** supply the correct monitored email, confirm telephone/WhatsApp, approve privacy wording, and test both handoffs on real devices.
2. **P0 — establish the live identity:** choose hosting, confirm the canonical domain and www/apex preference, then validate redirects, metadata, sitemap and robots on the deployed candidate.
3. **P0 — complete legal and rights approval:** publish Privacy/Terms only after professional review; approve every currently deployed visual asset.
4. **P1 — publish one evidence-rich case study:** challenge, direction, execution, rights-cleared imagery and client approval. Results remain optional unless evidenced.
5. **P1 — add founder credibility:** a concise approved biography and rights-cleared portrait will make the premium positioning feel accountable.
6. **P1 — clarify commercial expectations:** approved response time, fit criteria, project range and support boundary near Contact/Services.
7. **P2 — add an approved OG fallback and route previews:** this improves link-sharing quality once the canonical domain is known.

## Evidence limits

Screenshots do not prove physical-device behavior, email/WhatsApp delivery, screen-reader output, production hosting, network performance or legal accuracy. Those remain separate launch gates.
