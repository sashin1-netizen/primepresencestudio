What S&A is

Cars are not transport here.

They are obsession, problem-solving, late nights, parts hunting, fabrication, wiring diagrams, paint dust, first starts and the moment a project finally moves under its own power. That is the energy behind S&A Customs.

“We don’t modify cars. We sculpt them.”

Why it matters

The build is personal.

S&A sits inside the South African VW, stance, restoration and performance culture rather than outside it looking in. That changes the work. These are cars the team understands because they care about the same details the owners care about.

Not a service menu. A workshop for people who already know why the right wheel gap, the right engine note, the right bay and the right finish matter.
