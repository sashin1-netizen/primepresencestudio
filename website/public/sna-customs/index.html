The S&A story

Built on trust.
Known for the cars.

S&A Customs grew from Durban's enthusiast scene into a workshop associated with ambitious swaps, restoration work and standout Volkswagen builds. Third-party automotive coverage has repeatedly highlighted the workmanship, personalities and cars behind the name.
